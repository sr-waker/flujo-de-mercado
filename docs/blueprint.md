# **App Name**: MarketFlow

## Core Features:

- Offline-First Progressive Web App: Full PWA configuration for desktop installation and robust 100% offline functionality, utilizing LocalStorage for all data persistence, including historical sales and inventory.
- Intuitive Point of Sale (POS): An efficient interface for processing sales, including product search, quick-add buttons, dynamic price input for 'Carnicería' items, and interactive ticket management for extra charges.
- Comprehensive Inventory Control: Functionality to add, edit, and track products with current stock levels, define minimum stock thresholds, and display clear visual alerts for low inventory statuses.
- Purchase & Supplier Management: Tools to record supplier invoices, manage incoming stock orders, and update inventory levels seamlessly upon receipt of goods.
- AI Business Insights Assistant: An integrated AI tool, powered by Google Gemini, providing a concise analysis of business data like sales trends and stock alerts, along with motivational insights and actionable advice.
- Financial Reporting & Analytics: Dynamic reporting dashboard to visualize sales against expenses, calculate net profit, and filter data by 'Esta Semana', 'Este Mes', and 'Este Año' for comprehensive business overview.
- Shift & Cash Reconciliation: A 'Cerrar Caja' function within the user menu to finalize shifts, display total earnings for the turn, reset daily counters, and log historical shift data.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep Emerald Green (#299483), symbolizing growth and freshness against a professional backdrop.
- Background color: Charcoal Slate (#171E1D), providing a dark, sophisticated canvas with a subtle green undertone derived from the primary hue.
- Accent color: Vibrant Aqua Blue (#31BDE5), used for key interactive elements and highlights to provide energetic contrast and visual clarity.
- All text uses 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif font known for its modern, clean, and highly readable characteristics across headlines and body text.
- Utilize 'FontAwesome 6' for all icons, ensuring a consistent, professional, and scalable visual language throughout the application.
- Implement a clean, organized layout with prominent rounded borders on interactive elements and cards, complemented by soft, subtle shadows for depth and a contemporary feel.
- Subtle, fluid animations and transitions for navigation, alerts (e.g., pulsating low stock indicators), and interactive elements to enhance user engagement and provide immediate feedback without distraction.
